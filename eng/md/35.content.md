## Habakkuk 1:1 (id: 10035001001)

1I am Habakkuk the prophet. This is the message that Yahweh gave me in a vision.


## Habakkuk 1:2 (id: 10035001002)

2I said, “Yahweh, how long must I keep praying to you for help before you respond? I ask you to rescue us from people who are acting violently everywhere, but you do not rescue us!


## Habakkuk 1:3 (id: 10035001003)

3Why do you make me keep seeing people doing evil? Why do you not punish people who are doing what is wrong? I see people destroying things and acting violently, and there is fighting and quarreling everywhere.


## Habakkuk 1:4 (id: 10035001004)

4No one obeys your instruction, and judges never decide cases fairly. Wicked people always defeat righteous people in the courts, so that no one ever gets justice.”


## Habakkuk 1:5 (id: 10035001005)

5Yahweh replied to me, “Look at the completely amazing thing happening among the other nations; you will be astounded, because I am doing something during this time that you would not believe even if someone told you about it.


## Habakkuk 1:6 (id: 10035001006)

6I am making the Chaldeans powerful; they are fierce and swift soldiers. They will march across the entire earth and conquer countries that do not belong to them.


## Habakkuk 1:7 (id: 10035001007)

7They are terrifying people who do whatever they want and pay no attention to the laws of other countries.


## Habakkuk 1:8 (id: 10035001008)

8Their horses are faster than leopards and fiercer than wolves at night. Their horsemen gallop swiftly from distant places; they swoop down like eagles rushing to kill their prey.


## Habakkuk 1:9 (id: 10035001009)

9They all advance with the purpose of acting violently. They advance like a desert wind, gathering prisoners as numerous as grains of sand.


## Habakkuk 1:10 (id: 10035001010)

10They mock the kings and rulers of other countries and laugh at all the cities with high walls. They pile up dirt against those walls and climb up to capture those cities.


## Habakkuk 1:11 (id: 10035001011)

11They rush past like the wind and move on. But they are very guilty, because they think that their own military power is their god.”


## Habakkuk 1:12 (id: 10035001012)

12Then I said, “Yahweh, you have always been God from ancient times. You are my Holy One; we will not die. You have appointed the Chaldeans to judge us and punish us. You are our Rock, and you have sent them to correct us.


## Habakkuk 1:13 (id: 10035001013)

13You are pure, and you cannot endure seeing evil, so why are you tolerating treacherous people? Why do you say nothing when wicked people destroy people who are more righteous than they are?


## Habakkuk 1:14 (id: 10035001014)

14You treat us like fish in the sea or like other sea creatures that have no one to rule over them.


## Habakkuk 1:15 (id: 10035001015)

15The soldiers of Chaldea treat us like fish; they pull us out with hooks or catch us in their nets, and they rejoice and celebrate.


## Habakkuk 1:16 (id: 10035001016)

16They worship the weapons with which they capture people and offer sacrifices to them and burn incense in front of them. They say, ‘Those weapons have enabled us to become rich and eat expensive food.’


## Habakkuk 1:17 (id: 10035001017)

17Will you allow them to keep conquering people? Will you allow them to keep destroying people of other nations without showing any mercy?”


## Habakkuk 2:1 (id: 10035002001)

1After I said that, I said to myself, “I will climb up into my guard post, and stand there in my watchtower. I will wait there to find out what Yahweh will say, to know how I should answer.”


## Habakkuk 2:2 (id: 10035002002)

2Then Yahweh replied to me, “Write plainly on tablets what I am revealing to you in this vision, and then read it to a messenger so he can run with it to tell it to other people.


## Habakkuk 2:3 (id: 10035002003)

3In this vision I will talk about things that will happen when I decide. They will certainly happen in the future, I do not lie. You want those things to happen immediately, but they are not happening. But wait patiently for them to happen! When they occur, they will occur quickly, and not slowly.


## Habakkuk 2:4 (id: 10035002004)

4Think about the people in that proud nation! They are certainly not doing what is righteous. But people who are righteous will live because they faithfully do what I want them to do.


## Habakkuk 2:5 (id: 10035002005)

5Drunk armies are foolish, they cannot stop pillaging. No matter how much they kill and steal, they want more. They will conquer every people group they encounter.


## Habakkuk 2:6 (id: 10035002006)

6But soon all those whom they have captured will ridicule the soldiers from Babylonia! They will make fun of them, saying, ‘Terrible things will happen to you who have stolen things from other countries! You got many things by forcing people to give them to you. But you certainly will not keep those things for long!’


## Habakkuk 2:7 (id: 10035002007)

7Suddenly those you owe a debt to because you pillaged them will come to pillage you. You will be afraid. They will recapture all the things that you stole from them.


## Habakkuk 2:8 (id: 10035002008)

8You stole things from the people of many nations. You murdered people of many people groups, and you destroyed their land and their cities. So those who are still alive will steal valuable things from you.


## Habakkuk 2:9 (id: 10035002009)

9Terrible things will happen to you people of Babylonia who build big houses with money that you got by forcing others to give it to you. You are proud, and you think that your houses will be safe because you have built them in places where you can easily defend them.


## Habakkuk 2:10 (id: 10035002010)

10But because you have destroyed others, you have harmed yourself and disgraced your own family!


## Habakkuk 2:11 (id: 10035002011)

11The very stones in the walls of your houses and the wooden beams in your ceilings show that you did evil things to get them


## Habakkuk 2:12 (id: 10035002012)

12Terrible things will happen to you people of Babylonia who kill people in order to build cities, cities that you build by using money that you have gotten by committing crimes.


## Habakkuk 2:13 (id: 10035002013)

13But Yahweh, commander of the angel armies, has declared that fire will destroy everything built by people who do things like that; they will have worked hard uselessly.


## Habakkuk 2:14 (id: 10035002014)

14But in contrast, just as water fills the oceans, Yahweh will fill the earth with people who know how glorious he is.


## Habakkuk 2:15 (id: 10035002015)

15Terrible things will happen to you people of Babylonia, who cause people who live in nearby countries to become drunk. You force them to drink a lot of drugged wine until they are drunk and then they walk around naked because you are happy when you see that.


## Habakkuk 2:16 (id: 10035002016)

16But you are the ones whom Yahweh will soon disgrace instead of honor. Yahweh will make you drink so much wine that you will also stagger around drunk and naked. Yahweh will powerfully judge and curse you, and everyone will see that you are weak and not strong.


## Habakkuk 2:17 (id: 10035002017)

17You did violent things to the people in Lebanon, and you killed the wild animals there, so you will be punished severely for doing that. You have killed many people and you have destroyed their lands and their cities.


## Habakkuk 2:18 (id: 10035002018)

18An idol that someone carves or casts from metal does not help anyone at all; it only deceives the people who trust it. The person who makes an idol trusts something that he himself created, something that cannot even speak!


## Habakkuk 2:19 (id: 10035002019)

19Terrible things will happen to you who say to lifeless idols that are made of wood or rock, ‘Wake up!’ Stone idols certainly cannot tell you what you should do; they look nice because they are covered with silver and gold, but they are not alive.


## Habakkuk 2:20 (id: 10035002020)

20But Yahweh is in his holy temple; everyone on the earth should be silent in his presence!”


## Habakkuk 3:1 (id: 10035003001)

1Habakkuk the prophet prayed this prayer, set to the tune called Shigyonoth.


## Habakkuk 3:2 (id: 10035003002)

2Yahweh, I have heard reports about what you have done, and I am in awe of all you have done. Do again in our time what you did long ago! Even when you are angry with us, act mercifully toward us!


## Habakkuk 3:3 (id: 10035003003)

3God came from Teman, and the Holy One came from Mount Paran. His glory covered the sky, and praise for him filled the whole earth.


## Habakkuk 3:4 (id: 10035003004)

4He shone like the sunrise; his rays of light flashed from his hands, and that is where he conceals his awesome power.


## Habakkuk 3:5 (id: 10035003005)

5Sickness went ahead of him like a soldier, and burning disease followed behind him.


## Habakkuk 3:6 (id: 10035003006)

6He stopped and surveyed the earth. He looked at the nations and made them tremble. The mountains that have existed from the beginning of time collapsed; the ancient hills crumbled. He has always existed and always will.


## Habakkuk 3:7 (id: 10035003007)

7I saw that the people in the tents of Cushan were distressed, and the people of Midian were trembling like tent curtains.


## Habakkuk 3:8 (id: 10035003008)

8Yahweh, were you angry at the rivers and streams? Were you furious at the sea? No! You rode on your horses and on your chariots to defeat your enemies and rescue your people.


## Habakkuk 3:9 (id: 10035003009)

9You took your bow out of its cover, ready to shoot. You made ready your arrows, just as you had promised. You split the ground, and water gushed out in rivers.


## Habakkuk 3:10 (id: 10035003010)

10The mountains trembled when they saw you. A great flood of water rushed past. The ocean depths roared loudly and seemed to raise their hands high in the air.


## Habakkuk 3:11 (id: 10035003011)

11The sun and the moon stopped moving as your arrows flew through the sky like flashes of light and your shining spear gleamed brightly.


## Habakkuk 3:12 (id: 10035003012)

12You marched across the land in your anger, and you trampled the nations in your fury.


## Habakkuk 3:13 (id: 10035003013)

13You went out to rescue your people, to save the king you had chosen. You crushed the ruler of the wicked people, destroying his power from his crown to his very foundation.


## Habakkuk 3:14 (id: 10035003014)

14With the enemy’s own spear you killed their leader, who had come rushing like a whirlwind to scatter us and gloated like they were about to devour helpless people in hiding.


## Habakkuk 3:15 (id: 10035003015)

15You smashed through the sea on your horses, churning through the great surging waves.


## Habakkuk 3:16 (id: 10035003016)

16When I heard this, my stomach churned inside me; at the sound of it, my lips trembled. My bones felt like they were rotting, and my legs gave way beneath me. However, I will wait quietly for the time when disaster comes to the people who are attacking us.


## Habakkuk 3:17 (id: 10035003017)

17The fig trees may not blossom, and there may be no grapes on the vines. The olive trees may produce nothing, and the fields may yield no crops. The flocks of sheep and goats may disappear from the pastures, and there may be no cattle in the stalls.


## Habakkuk 3:18 (id: 10035003018)

18Even so, I will rejoice because I know Yahweh. I will be joyful because Yahweh is the God who saves me.


## Habakkuk 3:19 (id: 10035003019)

19Yahweh the Lord makes me strong. He makes me as sure\-footed as a deer and enables me to go safely to the high places. (This is for the choir director to use when the people sing this prayer with stringed instruments.)
